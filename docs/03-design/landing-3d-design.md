## Composition

The hero places the introduction and primary action on shaded foliage to the left. The Pine trunk enters from the bottom and converges toward the upper crown. Tall surrounding trees enter from the frame edges and form an enclosing canopy with irregular sky openings. The perspective camera is at standing height, looking almost straight up; the ground is outside the frame. One fixed, decorative WebGL canvas sits behind ordinary HTML. Scrolling is native; there is no camera navigation or scroll capture. Reading sections use opaque woodland surfaces in light/dark UI themes, interspersed with views of the forest. The return section uses a real render of the existing solo scene instead of the former line drawing.

Landing has its own camera and local scene preferences (`pyneath:landing-scene`). Its `/landing/pine-landing.glb` is generated by the shared forest generator with `--landing`, containing the ancient Pine and a ring of surrounding canopy trees without a session participant, gathering, bridge, paths, stream, lantern or small ground decorations. Marten and water-current animation are omitted from the landing renderer. A named crown group provides subtle foliage motion around the lower crown while the trunk and roots stay fixed. Shared phase lighting and rain remain, with a landing-only lighting/fog profile. Optional environment flags disable lantern lighting and river ripples for landing; their defaults preserve Camp and Solo. No new dependencies or remote assets were added.

## Controls and accessibility

- Primary actions: honey wood `#e1b77b`, dark brown text `#302318`, a lighter upper edge and short lower shadow.
- Secondary controls: pine green `#294b39`, cream text `#f4ecd9`. Selected example buttons use moss green and a visible check mark.
- All forest styles are scoped under `.landing.landing-forest`, including public menu/header/footer controls. Auth and application pages keep their existing styling.
- Native links, selects, details and the existing modal mobile menu retain keyboard behavior. Focus rings use cream over forest surfaces and dark green over light reading surfaces.
- Scene settings offer time, simulated weather, light mode and motion. Automatic time uses the device clock; automatic weather cycles every 20 minutes, with no location lookup or real-weather claim.
- Reduced motion disables decorative motion. Hidden documents stop rendering. A saved light preference or Save-Data avoids mounting the canvas. Loading/model failure/context loss retains HTML controls and a phase-matched poster, with a retry action after failure.
- Sixteen WebP posters cover four times of day, clear/overcast skies and desktop/portrait compositions; total 926,094 bytes. Static weather is represented by a non-animated tint/haze or sparse rain overlay.
