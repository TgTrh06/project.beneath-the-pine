# Pilot Crossover Protocol

## Research question

Does the full Beneath Pine platform reduce time from a participant declaring a stuck moment to their first action, compared with a minimal self-monitoring logger?

This is a feasibility/usability pilot, not a clinical trial or evidence of treatment efficacy.

## Participants and conditions

- Recruit 5–8 voluntary adults from the student's network. ADHD diagnosis is neither requested nor verified.
- Each participant completes two seven-day conditions; assignment alternates `control_first` and `intervention_first`.
- Control: timestamped `stuck` / `start` logger and 1–5 friction ratings only.
- Intervention: full Beneath Pine core loop, including the project-owned model when available.
- Target: at least three valid stuck-to-start sessions in each condition.

## Collected research metadata

`participant_code`, condition, session timestamps, time-to-start, friction before/after, and focus outcome. The participant's account retains raw task/Brain Dump data under normal product policy; the research export must not include it.

## Analysis

Report each participant's median time-to-start per condition, paired difference, overall median, session count, friction change, and focus completion rate. Any statistical test is exploratory because of the small sample. Describe missing sessions and withdrawals rather than imputing them.

## Consent and retention

Use separate opt-in research consent. Participation may be withdrawn without beta-account consequences. Delete participant mapping and detailed study metadata 30 days after defense; retain aggregate tables only.
