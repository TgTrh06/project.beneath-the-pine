# Datasheet — Source Research Boundary

External sources may be consulted only to identify abstract coverage dimensions such as ambiguity, correction, setup friction, safe refusal or small-step planning. They are not a source of training rows.

Do not copy, translate, paraphrase or retain external utterances, social posts, therapy text or user content in the Pine Assistance dataset. Every train/eval row must be newly fictional, explicitly consented and de-identified, or substantially rewritten synthetic material reviewed by a Vietnamese reviewer.

Current Pine Assistance coverage is defined in the [Dataset Specification](../docs/05-machine-learning/dataset-spec.md). Record only source name, license check date and abstract dimension in a private research register when source research occurs.
